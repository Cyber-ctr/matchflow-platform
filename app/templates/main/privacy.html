Privacy

MatchFlow is designed around data minimisation, account security and user control.

What we collect

Account details, profile information, opportunities, applications, messages and platform activity needed to operate the service.

How we use it

We use data to provide matching, communication, application tracking, trust features, security and platform analytics.

Your choices

Users can update their profile information and contact the MatchFlow team about account or data requests.

Security

Production deployments should use HTTPS, secure cookies, strong secrets, PostgreSQL credentials kept outside source code, and restricted administrative access.
